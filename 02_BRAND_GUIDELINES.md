# Lolo Cupcakes --- Brand Guidelines (Initial Direction)

## Brand Positioning

Lolo Cupcakes is a premium yet welcoming dessert brand. The identity
should communicate handmade quality, celebration, care, indulgence, and
trust. Avoid making the brand appear limited to cupcakes; cakes and
broader dessert collections must feel equally important.

## Visual Direction

Elegant, playful, warm, premium, editorial product photography, clean
layouts, restrained decoration.

## Proposed Palette

These are starting tokens, not final approved values: - Blush Pink:
`#E8B9C3` - Vanilla Cream: `#F8F1E8` - Cocoa Brown: `#4B302B` -
Espresso: `#2B1D1A` - Champagne Gold: `#C6A46A` - White: `#FFFFFF`

Use cocoa/espresso for readable text. Pink and gold are accents, not
substitutes for accessible contrast. Validate all text/background
combinations.

## Typography

Select Arabic and Latin font families with compatible visual character
and strong readability. Candidate direction: a clean Arabic sans-serif
paired with a refined Latin sans-serif. Verify font licensing and Arabic
glyph quality before bundling.

## Logo Deliverables

-   Primary horizontal logo.
-   Stacked logo.
-   Compact mark / favicon.
-   Light and dark background variants.
-   Monochrome version.
-   Clear-space and minimum-size rules.

Do not invent a final logo or claim brand assets are approved. Create
draft concepts and request owner approval.

## Photography

-   Real product photography is preferred.
-   Consistent lighting and background.
-   Accurate representation of product size, texture, and decoration.
-   Avoid generic stock imagery as final product photos.
-   Use placeholders clearly marked as placeholders until real assets
    are supplied.

## UI Principles

-   Product imagery leads.
-   Spacious cards and clear prices.
-   Minimal decorative animation.
-   Consistent button hierarchy.
-   Strong focus and hover states.
-   Mobile-first layouts.
-   Arabic-first RTL composition.
-   Avoid excessive gradients, glassmorphism, and clutter.

## Design Tokens

Create centralized tokens for color, typography, spacing, radius,
shadows, breakpoints, and component states. Do not scatter raw color
values across components.
